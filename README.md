# Raging Page 🔥

Free screenwriting in your browser. No account, no subscription, no ads. Your screenplays stay on your computer and belong to you.

👉 **Try it:** https://mtaofficiel69-debug.github.io/ragingpage/

Available in **English** and **Français**.

## Why?

Screenwriting software is expensive and most of it wants a monthly subscription. Raging Page is a free, open alternative.

## Features

- Industry-standard pagination
- Series with seasons and episodes
- Structure cards with templates
- Colour revisions and version comparison
- Character reports and actor sides
- Title page (you can even draw on it)
- Find & replace, synonyms, voice dictation
- Focus mode, typewriter mode, dark theme
- Import: text, Fountain, Final Draft (.fdx), PDF
- Export: .fdx, Fountain, text, PDF
- Automatic saving, full backup file, optional Google Drive

## Your data

Everything is saved in your browser, on your computer. No account, no analytics. Google Drive is optional. Note: fonts and PDF tools load from the internet.

## Honest warning

100% vibe-coded (built with AI, directed by me). It's not perfect and has bugs. That's why it's open source: **help me make it better.**
