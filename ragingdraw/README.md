# Raging Draw 🎨

Design your film posters from A to Z, for free, in your browser. Same spirit as Raging Page: no account, no subscription, no ads. Your posters stay on your computer and belong to you.

👉 **Try it:** https://mtaofficiel69-debug.github.io/ragingpage/ragingdraw/

Available in **English** and **Français**.

## Open it

- Online, with the link above.
- Or on your computer: double-click `index.html` in this folder. No installation needed.

## Features

- 8 poster templates (thriller, horror, sci-fi, festival, comedy, vintage, minimalist, neon) or a blank canvas
- Sizes: US one-sheet 27 × 40 in, French poster 120 × 160 cm, 40 × 60 cm, A3, A4, Instagram, story, banner, square, custom
- Text with 100+ fonts, gradients, outline, neon glow, shadow, letter spacing
- Ready-made poster elements: billing block, release date, festival laurels, critic quote, rating
- Photos: photo frames, crop, brightness, contrast, black & white, tint, edge fade, blend modes
- Freehand drawing: brush, chisel marker, airbrush, neon, stylus pressure, and an eraser you can always undo
- Shapes, vector decor (film strip, sunburst, splatter, drips…), film grain, vignette
- Layers, alignment, snapping, undo / redo, keyboard shortcuts
- Export: PNG, JPEG and PDF up to 300 dpi (actual size for the printer), plus a `.rdraw` project file

## Your data

Everything is saved in your browser, on your computer. No account, no analytics. Note: fonts load from the internet.
